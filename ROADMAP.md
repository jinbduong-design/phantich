# ProductPulse Roadmap

## Product principle

Every important conclusion should expose:
- **Score** — what the current evidence says
- **Confidence** — how strong and broad that evidence is
- **Evidence** — the observations behind the score
- **Missing data** — what is still unknown
- **Next action** — the cheapest useful way to reduce uncertainty

No evidence means no authoritative score.

## v0.1.0 — Product Intelligence Foundation
- Centralized versioning.
- Responsive evaluation UI.
- Initial score/confidence separation.
- Conservative AI prompt.

## v0.2.0 — Evidence-based Evaluation
- Remove all fictional seed products and simulated reviews.
- Start the production workspace empty.
- Clear/ignore legacy seeded localStorage.
- Add structured validation evidence to every product.
- Calculate every pillar from multiple independent signals.
- Show criterion-level factors, weights, sample sufficiency and missing evidence.
- Mark low-evidence products as **Chưa đủ dữ liệu**.
- Prevent AI from changing scores or manufacturing market/customer evidence.
- Recalculate imported legacy products from real evidence.

## v0.3.0 — Validation Workspace
- Evidence log with source, date and attachment/link.
- Interview records and tagged insights.
- Experiment objects with hypothesis, metric, threshold and result.
- Score history tied to evidence changes.
- Validation task queue generated from the highest-uncertainty assumptions.

## v0.4.0 — Economics & GTM
- Revenue, COGS, gross margin, CAC, payback and repeat purchase.
- Channel-level acquisition experiments.
- Stage/category-specific configurable benchmarks.
- Cohort/retention support where relevant.

## v0.5.0 — Research
- Sourced competitor and market evidence.
- Source URL, publication date and freshness.
- Fact vs assumption vs AI hypothesis labeling.
- Research notes reusable across products.

## v0.6.0 — Decision System
- Evaluation snapshots.
- Assumption register.
- Experiment history.
- Decision journal and change explanations.
- Portfolio comparison constrained by evidence quality.

## v1.0.0 — Product Intelligence
Idea → evidence → validation → economics → distribution → risk → decision → experiment plan.
