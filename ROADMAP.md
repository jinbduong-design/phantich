# ProductPulse Roadmap

ProductPulse is moving from a report-style product scoring prototype toward a decision system that separates **score, evidence, confidence and next action**.

## v0.1.0 — Product Intelligence Foundation
- Establish a single app version source.
- Make the core evaluation UI denser and easier to scan on desktop and mobile.
- Remove false precision from default scoring.
- Surface data confidence and missing inputs next to the score.
- Keep the current local-first workflow and existing product analysis modules.

## v0.2.0 — UX Foundation
- Split the oversized app shell into smaller route/state modules.
- Refine desktop workspace and mobile navigation independently.
- Reduce repeated labels, cards and explanatory copy.
- Add compact empty/loading/error states and better one-hand mobile actions.
- Standardize spacing, typography and responsive breakpoints.

## v0.3.0 — Evaluation Engine
- Separate Product Score from Evidence Confidence.
- Add criterion-level evidence, assumptions and unknowns.
- Score customer pain, willingness to pay, differentiation, economics, execution risk and market access.
- Prevent missing evidence from being interpreted as a high score.
- Add configurable weights by product stage.

## v0.4.0 — Validation
- Turn unknowns into validation tasks.
- Track interviews, landing-page tests, preorder tests, pricing tests and experiment results.
- Recalculate confidence from real validation evidence.
- Show what changed since the previous evaluation.

## v0.5.0 — Product Research
- Attach competitors, market signals and customer evidence with source + date.
- Distinguish sourced facts from AI inference.
- Add evidence freshness and source quality.
- Build reusable research notes for each product.

## v0.6.0 — Decision System
- Score history and evaluation snapshots.
- Assumption log and experiment history.
- Decision journal: continue, revise, test or pause.
- Compare products on evidence quality as well as headline score.

## v1.0.0 — Product Intelligence
- Idea → research → validation → economics → risk → recommendation → experiment plan.
- Explain every material score change.
- Produce concise decision briefs instead of long generic AI reports.
- Support reliable long-term product portfolio review.

## Product principles
1. No high-confidence conclusion without evidence.
2. Missing data lowers confidence; it must not magically increase the score.
3. AI suggestions are hypotheses until validated.
4. Mobile is for quick review/action; desktop is for deeper analysis.
5. Prefer a few decision-critical signals over dense dashboards.
